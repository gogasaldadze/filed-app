Privacy Policy

PDF Converter, by We Up LLC. Effective 5 November 2026.

PDF Converter scans and imports documents, reads them on your device and files them by type, sender and date. It can also sign, mark up, convert, merge and compress them on your device. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Android app. Contact for privacy questions: support@weup.uk.

Your documents

Scanned pages, imported files, recognized text, titles, filing details and signature images you draw are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive them and we have no copy. The app turns off Android backup for this data, so it is not copied to your Google account backup. Uninstalling the app removes it.

A document leaves your device only when you choose to share or save it elsewhere. It then goes to the app or location you pick, under that recipient's terms.

The app does not ask for the camera, photo library or storage permissions. The scanner runs in Google Play services, and imports go through Android's own pickers, which give the app only the items you select. Word and text files you import, and PDFs you open in the app from another app, are converted or copied on your device.

Data sent off your device

Subscriptions, through RevenueCat and Google Play

The app has no accounts. To sell and check Pro, it uses RevenueCat, Inc. and Google Play billing. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history, and your locale and currency, along with basic technical details needed to make the request. We use this to provide your subscription and to understand subscription numbers. Payment details are handled by Google Play and we never see them. This is required for purchases and restoring them. See the RevenueCat privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Text recognition and scanning, through Google Play services

Text recognition and the document scanner are Google ML Kit features that process your pages on the device. Google states that ML Kit may send diagnostic and usage information to Google, such as device and app information, device or other identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input and output size, feature version, event type and error codes, to keep the features working and to measure performance. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. The app shows no ads and has no advertising ID. We do not use your documents to train models or for any other purpose, because we do not receive them.

Security

Data sent by the app and these services travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Documents on your device are protected by Android's app storage isolation. Anyone who can unlock your device and open the app can see them.

Retention and deletion

Documents stay on your device until you delete them, use Settings, then Delete all my documents, or uninstall the app. Subscription records are kept while they are needed to provide your subscription and to meet accounting and legal duties. To request deletion of subscription records, follow the steps on the data deletion page. Records we must keep for legal, fraud prevention or security reasons are kept only for as long as that reason applies.

Children

PDF Converter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child's data has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data. Contact us at support@weup.uk to use these rights.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
