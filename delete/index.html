Delete your data

PDF Converter, by We Up LLC.

PDF Converter has no accounts or sign in. There are two kinds of data, and you can remove both without keeping the app installed.

Documents on your device

We never receive your documents, so only you can delete them.

  • In the app, open Settings, then Delete all my documents.
  • Or uninstall the app, which removes every document, page, its text and saved signatures.

Subscription records

If you bought Pro, RevenueCat holds an anonymous ID and your purchase history for us. To request deletion, even if you no longer have the app:

  1. Find your Google Play order number, which starts with GPA. It is in your Google Play purchase email and at Google Play order history.
  2. Email it to support@weup.uk with the subject "PDF Converter data deletion".

The order number lets us find your anonymous ID without asking for anything else. We delete the record and ask RevenueCat to delete its copy within 30 days, and confirm by email. If you never bought Pro, the anonymous ID holds no purchases and we cannot link it to you.

What may be kept

Order and tax records required by law are kept by Google and by us for as long as that duty lasts. Diagnostic data Google receives through ML Kit is handled by Google, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Cancelling comes first

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel at Google Play subscriptions before you make a request.
